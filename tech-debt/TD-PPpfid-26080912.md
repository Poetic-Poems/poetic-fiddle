Noticed during review of PR #250 (TD-PPpfid-26080901, Terms of Service
termination copy). `src/app/privacy/page.tsx`'s "Saving and sharing poems"
section (around line 84) has described the self-service "Danger zone"
account-deletion flow (My poems dashboard, W13/PR #185) since PR #193
(TD-PPpfid-26080302), keeping the mailto only as a fallback for a poet who
can't sign in. The page's own "Your rights" section, further down (around
lines 152–164), was left as it was: "You can ask us to export or delete your
data, or delete your account entirely, at any time by emailing
warwick@datumprocess.co.nz" — no mention of the self-service path, so the
same page now describes two different ways to delete an account depending
on which section a reader reaches first.

TD-PPpfid-26080302 explicitly judged "Your rights" out of scope at the time
("accurate as written... export genuinely has no self-service path"), which
was true for export; it did not carry the same judgement for the account
deletion clause folded into the same sentence.

Fix: update the "Your rights" section's account-deletion clause to mention
the self-service Danger Zone flow (My poems dashboard), keeping the mailto
as the fallback for a poet who can't sign in — matching the "Saving and
sharing poems" section's existing wording. Export has no self-service path,
so that half of the sentence is unaffected.
