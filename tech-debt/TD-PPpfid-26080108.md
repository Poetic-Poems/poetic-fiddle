*Filed 2026-08-01, found while bumping `poetic` to v6.3.0 (#146).*

poetic clamps a postscript to a preview budget and offers a "See more" control
to lift it: `_poem-content.pug` emits a `.postscript-content` carrying
`style="--preview-lines: 5"` next to a `button.postscript-toggle`, and
`public/poetic.css` turns that into
`max-height: calc(var(--preview-lines, 5) * 1lh)` with `overflow: hidden` and a
fade mask. Three pieces of `public/poetic.js` make it behave: a delegated click
handler that flips `aria-expanded` and toggles `.postscript-expanded` (which
lifts the clamp), `evaluatePostscriptPreview()` on `DOMContentLoaded`, and a
debounced re-run of the latter on resize.

Fiddle never loads `poetic.js` — `PoemPreview` grants its iframe no
`allow-scripts` at all, and `SharedPoemView` injects only the stylesheet — so
each of those has to be mirrored here or go missing. `wirePoemToggles`
(`src/components/PoemPreview.tsx`, wired from both views' `onLoad`) covers the
click handler as of #146. Two gaps remain.

**1. The control is offered even when there is nothing to reveal.**
`evaluatePostscriptPreview()` measures rendered content against the
`--preview-lines` budget and adds `.postscript-no-preview` — which lifts the
clamp and hides the control — when truncation would hide a line or less. Fiddle
runs none of it, so a two-line postscript is still masked and still offers "See
more", which expands to exactly the text already on screen. Fixing it needs the
same layout measurement (`getBoundingClientRect()` against the line-height
budget, per poetic's implementation) run inside the iframe once it has laid
out, plus a debounced resize listener; both views already reach the iframe
document through `allow-same-origin`, so no new capability is involved. This
one predates #146: v6.1.1's `poetic.js` carried it too and Fiddle never ran it.

**2. The share page's postscript is no longer readable without client-side
JS.** AC84 has the share page viewable with JS off, and it used to hold here
for free: until poetic v6.2.0 the control was an `input.postscript-toggle-cb`
plus a `label`, and `.postscript-toggle-cb:checked ~ .postscript-content`
lifted the clamp with no script at all. poetic #79 replaced that with a scripted
`button` for keyboard operability, so with JS disabled a postscript longer than
the budget is now truncated with no way to read the rest. This is not fixable
by wiring alone, and `poetic.css` is consumed as-is per `CLAUDE.md`'s
single-source-of-truth rule, so it needs a decision between:

- an upstream change giving the clamp a scriptless escape (a `:target` or
  `<details>` fallback, or defaulting the clamp off and applying it from
  `poetic.js`, which would degrade to "fully expanded" rather than "truncated");
- or accepting that the share page renders postscripts unclamped, by adding
  `.postscript-no-preview` in `sanitizeSharedPoemHtml`
  (`src/lib/render-share.ts`) — cheap, and it also disposes of gap 1 for that
  view, at the cost of long postscripts no longer being collapsible there.

Whichever way it goes, the preview and the share page should not disagree.

`wirePoemToggles`'s comment in `src/components/PoemPreview.tsx` names this
item; remove that reference when resolving.
