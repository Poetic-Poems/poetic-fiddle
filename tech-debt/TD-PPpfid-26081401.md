Noticed during review of PR #319 (issue #315). `wirePoemToggles`
(`src/lib/poem-toggles.ts`) intercepts clicks on `a[href]` inside the two
`srcDoc` preview frames and reopens the link with `window.open` from the
parent realm, because letting the click renavigate the frame trips the app
shell's `frame-src` directive. It treats every `click` event alike, which
leaves two gestures behaving differently from an ordinary link:

- **Ctrl/Cmd/Shift+click.** These dispatch a plain `click`, so they are
  intercepted and become an ordinary `window.open(…, "_blank")` — a
  foreground tab, where the poet asked for a background tab (Ctrl/Cmd) or a
  new window (Shift).
- **Middle-click.** This dispatches `auxclick`, not `click`, so it is not
  intercepted at all and still renavigates the frame — the original
  `frame-src` violation from issue #315, surviving on that one gesture.

The obvious fix — skip non-primary and modified clicks, and let the browser
handle them — is not obviously safe. `PoemPreview`'s iframe is
`sandbox="allow-same-origin"` with no `allow-popups`, so a browser-handled
Ctrl+click may be suppressed as a sandboxed auxiliary navigation and do
nothing at all, which is worse than a foreground tab. Settling that needs a
real browser against the sandbox, which is also why it was not decided in
#319: the reviewing node had no working headless browser.

Fix: determine, in a real browser, whether a modified click and a middle
click open a tab from inside each of the two sandboxes
(`PoemPreview`: `allow-same-origin`; `SharedPoemView`:
`allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-popups`, which does permit them).
Then either let modified/auxiliary clicks fall through to the browser where
the sandbox allows it, or handle `auxclick` alongside `click` so middle-click
gets the same `window.open` treatment. `docs/CSP-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md` is the
gate for verifying whichever way it goes.
