Noticed while implementing TD-PPpfid-26072433 (poet data export script and
the export/delete runbook). `src/app/privacy/page.tsx`'s "Saving and
sharing poems" section still reads: "To delete your entire account ... email
warwick@datumprocess.co.nz" — written before self-service account deletion
(`DELETE /api/account/delete`, W13, PR #185) shipped. The "Your rights"
section a little further down is accurate as written (it offers export *or*
delete by email, and export genuinely has no self-service path), but the
"Saving and sharing poems" section's specific claim that account deletion
requires emailing the maintainer is now stale — poets can delete their own
account from the app.

Fix: update that paragraph to mention the in-app self-service delete action,
keeping the mailto as the fallback for someone who can't sign in. Out of
scope for TD-PPpfid-26072433, which only touched internal/maintainer-facing
docs, not the public Privacy Policy page's copy.
