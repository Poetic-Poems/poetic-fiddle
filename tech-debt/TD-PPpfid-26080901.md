*Filed 2026-08-09, from the 2026-08-08 project review (R-01, F-UX-02, F-DOC-01).*
Severity: Medium. `src/app/terms/page.tsx`'s "Termination" section (around
line 147) says a poet can delete their account "by emailing
warwick@datumprocess.co.nz, as set out in our Privacy Policy" — but the
Privacy Policy no longer says that: `src/app/privacy/page.tsx` was updated
by PR #193 (2026-08-03) to describe the self-service "Danger zone" deletion
flow that shipped the same day in W13 (PR #185), with the mailto kept only
as a fallback for a poet who can't sign in. PR #193's fix was scoped to the
Privacy Policy page alone and never touched Terms, which still carries the
pre-W13 sentence (last touched 2026-07-28, five days before W13 shipped).
The two legal pages now disagree on the same fact, and Terms — the page
about account termination specifically — is the one steering a
non-technical poet away from the one-click self-service flow that already
exists.

Fix: update the Terms of Service "Termination" section to match the
Privacy Policy's current wording (self-service Danger Zone first, mailto
as the fallback for someone who can't sign in), and bump the page's
`lastUpdated` date. See
`reviews/project-review-2026-08-08/04-improvement-prompts.md` R-01.
